PhilAI 2026

Philology Meets AI: Deciphering Rare Scripts

The 2026 workshop was held on 4 May 2026 at Pärlan, Building 4, Albano, Stockholm University, Stockholm.

The workshop brought together researchers in philology, palaeography, epigraphy and artificial intelligence to examine how computational methods can support the analysis and decipherment of rare and undeciphered scripts.
