Submission details

We invite submissions to PhilAI 2027 for presentation at the workshop.

Regular papers

Regular papers may be up to 10 pages, including appendices and excluding references.

Regular papers should describe substantial, original and unpublished research.

Short papers

Short papers may be up to 4 pages, including appendices and excluding references.

Suitable contributions include:

  • focused research contributions
  • project presentations
  • system demonstrations
  • research tools
  • work in progress
  • negative results
  • surveys
  • tutorials
  • opinion pieces

Abstract submissions

Authors may alternatively submit an abstract without publication as a full paper.

Presentation formats

Accepted papers and abstracts may be presented as oral presentations, posters or demonstrations. Authors may indicate their preferred format, but the final decision will be made by the PhilAI 2027 Organisation Committee.

Review process

All submissions will receive at least two double-blind reviews by experts in the field.

Submission system

Submit via EasyChair

Attendance

At least one author of each accepted paper must participate in the workshop.
