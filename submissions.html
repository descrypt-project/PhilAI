Submission details

We invite submissions to PhilAI 2027 for presentation at the workshop.

Regular papers

Regular papers may be up to 10 pages, including appendices and excluding references. These papers should describe substantial, original and unpublished research.

Short papers

Short papers may be up to 4 pages, including appendices and excluding references.

Suitable contributions include:

  • focused research contributions
  • project presentations
  • system demonstrations
  • research tools
  • work in progress
  • negative results
  • surveys
  • tutorials
  • opinion pieces

Abstract submissions

Authors may alternatively submit an abstract or extended abstract for presentation without publication as a full paper. Abstract and extended-abstract submissions should be non-anonymous.

Presentation formats

Accepted papers and abstracts may be presented as oral presentations, posters or demonstrations. Authors may indicate their preferred format, but the final decision will be made by the PhilAI 2027 Organisation Committee.

Review process

Regular and short papers submitted for publication in the proceedings will undergo formal double-blind peer review and will receive at least two reviews by experts in the field.

Abstracts and extended abstracts will be submitted non-anonymously and will be assessed by the PhilAI 2027 Organisation Committee for inclusion in the programme.

Attendance

At least one author of each accepted paper must participate in the workshop.
