Keynotes

We are delighted to announce that Isabelle Marthot-Santaniello and Richard Sproat will be the keynote speakers at PhilAI 2027. Their expertise spans digital palaeography, papyrology, computational linguistics, artificial intelligence and the study of writing systems.

Isabelle Marthot-Santaniello

Isabelle Marthot-Santaniello is a professor at the University of Basel, specialising in papyrology, digital humanities and computational palaeography. Her research explores the use of digital methods to study ancient handwritten documents and to investigate historical scripts and scribal practices.

University of Basel profile

Richard Sproat

Richard Sproat is a computational linguist and research scientist at Sakana AI in Tokyo. His work encompasses natural language processing, artificial intelligence and the study of writing and symbol systems. His research also includes computational models of the early evolution of writing and the statistical properties of non-linguistic symbol systems.
