Venue

The conference will take place at Piperska Muren (Address: Scheelegatan 14, Stockholm), a historic seventeenth-century malmgård (suburban manor house) on Kungsholmen in central Stockholm.

Today, Piperska Muren is a modern conference venue that retains its historic character and offers access to a beautiful, lush garden. The venue includes a banquet hall accommodating up to 250 guests. Lunch and coffee breaks will be provided during the conference.
