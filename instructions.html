Author instructions

All submissions must use the official PhilAI 2027 style files.

Templates will be provided for:

  • LaTeX — preferred
  • Microsoft Word

Anonymous submission

Submissions must be anonymous and must not reveal the identity of the authors on the title page or through self-references.

Submissions that do not follow these requirements may be returned without review.

File format

Papers must be submitted digitally as PDF files.

The official PhilAI 2027 LaTeX and Word templates will be published on this page before submissions open.
