Author instructions

All submissions must use the official PhilAI 2027 style files.

Templates are available for:

  • LaTeX template — preferred
  • Microsoft Word template

Anonymity

Regular and short papers submitted for publication in the proceedings must be anonymous and must not reveal the identity of the authors on the title page or through self-references.

Abstract submissions should be non-anonymous. These submissions will be assessed by the PhilAI 2027 Organisation Committee for inclusion in the programme.

File format

Papers must be submitted digitally as PDF files.
